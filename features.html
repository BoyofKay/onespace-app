Everything the two of you need, nothing you don’t.

OneSpace is organised the way a couple actually lives: what is happening today, what you want to remember, what you need to coordinate, how you stay close, and how you like things set up.

WHAT MAKES ONESPACE DIFFERENT

More than emotional connection. A life you build together.

Love letters and daily gestures matter. What makes OneSpace different is what happens beyond them: share ideas from other apps, edit together, compare options, discuss decisions and put your plans into action.

01

Share anywhere. Keep one list together.

Share from another app and OneSpace suggests where the item belongs — a shared watchlist, a place, an option or a gift idea. Both of you know where to find it.

Today

Open the app, know what matters.

Today brings together what your partner sent, what needs your answer, and the next seven days — shaped by whether you live together or apart.

  • Since you were away — gestures, letters and updates you missed.
  • Needs your attention — votes, decisions and items assigned to you.
  • Two clocks & weather — when you live apart, see both cities at once.
  • Quick add — a reminder, to-do, note, place or trip in two taps.
  • Your shortcuts — pin the screens you use most.
Remember

Your shared memory, organised.

Counters for every special date, your places on a map, notes, contact cards and memories that become scrapbooks.

  • Counters — days, weeks, months and years — the date itself and its anniversary, with progress bars.
  • Linked dates — see the time between two moments, like first date → wedding.
  • Our places — been there and bucket list, pinned on a map, shared from Maps.
  • Memories & scrapbooks — stories, daily selfies of both of you, save as PDF.
  • Notes & contact cards — rich notes and the details you always need — sizes, the doctor, the plumber.
Coordinate

Decide together, without the back-and-forth.

A shared calendar, lists and reminders — plus activities where you each mark the options you like and decide together.

  • Calendar — one calendar for both of you, sent one way to your phone calendar if you like.
  • To-do lists & reminders — assign to one of you or both, with due dates and repeats.
  • Activities — compare options, vote, talk it through with voice notes and @mentions, then decide.
Trips

Every trip in one place — flights to packing.

Flights, stays, a shared packing list and private travel documents only the two of you can open.

  • Flights — routes, stops, baggage and prices for both directions.
  • Packing — who brings what, with progress.
  • Documents — bookings and tickets stored privately.
Occasions

Birthdays and anniversaries, planned in time.

Gift ideas with prices and ratings, a budget that tracks what is left, and a reminder to start early. Keep it private for a surprise.

  • Gift ideas — considering, reserved, bought — never the same gift twice.
  • Occasion budget — see what is spent and what is left.
  • Only me — surprises stay hidden from your partner.

SMART DETAILS

Find it fast. Know what matters.

Your shared life needs more than another list of alerts. OneSpace keeps information and daily rhythms in context.

GLOBAL SEARCH

One search across your shared space.

Forgot where you saved that link, note, place, trip or decision? Search across the information you can access, then open the original item. Private content stays protected by your account permissions.

  • Find information without opening every list
  • Searchable categories and filtered results
  • Respect private and shared visibility

THOUGHTFUL NOTIFICATIONS

Notifications that know your rhythm.

OneSpace uses the work and sleep schedule you set, including days off. Non-urgent partner pushes can wait while you sleep and arrive as a morning summary; urgent reminders can still alert you. Your usual working hours also help the app understand your daily routine.

  • Sleep-aware quiet hours and morning summary
  • Work schedule and days-off awareness
  • Grouped updates with context and direct links
Me

Set it up your way.

Nine themes, Arabic or English, notifications grouped by day, privacy controls and a full export.

  • Notifications that explain themselves — who, what and when, grouped by day.
  • Help without signing in — contact us even if you are locked out.
