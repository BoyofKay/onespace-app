Home › Why OneSpace

Beyond connection: plan and decide together.

Emotional connection is only part of a shared life. OneSpace brings your ideas, comparisons, two-way discussions, decisions and plans together in one private space for two.

WHAT MAKES ONESPACE DIFFERENT

More than emotional connection. A life you build together.

Love letters and daily gestures matter. What makes OneSpace different is what happens beyond them: share ideas from other apps, edit together, compare options, discuss decisions and put your plans into action.

01

Share anywhere. Keep one list together.

Share from another app and OneSpace suggests where the item belongs — a shared watchlist, a place, an option or a gift idea. Both of you know where to find it.

Explore smart sharing ↗
02

Two people. One evolving decision.

Both partners can contribute to shared activities: add or edit details, discuss options, see changes and keep the final choice beside the conversation.

Explore shared decisions ↗
03

Plan the whole journey, not just the date.

Compare prices and specifications, organise flights and packing, and prepare occasions with gift ideas and budgets — all connected to the plan.

Explore trip planning ↗
04

Emotional connection — and a life beyond it.

Letters, love gestures, check-ins and a feelings journal keep you close. OneSpace goes further: planning, discussion, comparisons and decisions happen in the same space.

The real comparison

Separate tools or one connected space?

Chats, notes and calendars are useful on their own. OneSpace is designed to keep a couple’s decisions and follow-up alongside the details.

The usual mixchat, notes, two calendars, spreadsheets, photosOneSpaceone private app for two
Plans and decisionsA chat thread that scrolls away✓ Activities with options, votes, a conversation and a recorded decision
Lists in other appsOne per app, per person — 20+ lists✓ One shared list per kind, from any app
Links from shops, Netflix, MapsSent and forgotten✓ Shared into the right list in one tap
CalendarTwo calendars, two truths✓ One shared calendar + public holidays
RemindersOnly for yourself✓ For me, my partner or both
TripsTabs, screenshots, two inboxes✓ Flight comparison, itinerary, private documents, packing
Birthdays & giftsRemembered too late✓ Gift ideas, budget, delivery warnings, private surprises
What to watch40 minutes of scrolling✓ Shared watchlists, mutual picks, a suggestion
Photos & memories14,000 photos, no story✓ Stories, scrapbooks, daily selfie, PDF
Special datesA note somewhere✓ Counters with progress, anniversaries and linked dates
Love & attentionLost between errands✓ A little love, sealed letters, daily check-ins
PrivacyMixed with work, groups and ads✓ Two people only. No ads. No data selling.
