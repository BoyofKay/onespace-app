Delete your account and data

You are always in control. Here is exactly what happens.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Me → Your data and account.
  2. Optional: Export my data first.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

What is deleted

Your account, your profile and the items only you created are deleted. If your partner stays, the shared space and what they created remain theirs. If you are the last member, the whole space is deleted, including its photos and files.

Can’t sign in?

Send a request below — or email privacy@onespace.live — from the email on your account. We will confirm it is you, then delete the account and reply.
