Palabras, imágenes y colores listos para usar para amigos, evaluadores, tiendas de aplicaciones y la prensa. Todo lo que aparece aquí puede servir para hablar de OneSpace.

Palabras

Cópialos tal como están. El árabe aparece cuando cambias de idioma.

una linea

OneSpace — the private app for the life you share, near or far.

Breve descripción (tienda, 80 caracteres)

Plans, reminders, trips, letters and memories for two — private and calm.

Descripción larga

OneSpace is one private space for you and your partner. See your shared day in Today, keep counters for your special dates and your places on a map, plan trips and occasions together, and stay close with little gestures, sealed letters and a daily check-in. It adapts to couples who live together and couples who live apart, works in English and Arabic, has nine themes, and never shows ads or sells data.

Mensaje a un amigo

We have been testing OneSpace — a private app just for couples: shared plans, reminders, trips and little gestures. It is free in early access: https://onespace.live/join/
