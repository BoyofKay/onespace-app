Privacy policy

In short: your space is private to you and your partner, we don’t sell data or show ads, and you can download or delete everything at any time.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

OneSpace is an app for couples. In this policy “we” means the OneSpace team. You can reach us any time from Support, from the sign-in screen of the app, or from Me → Help in the app.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, email address, language, time zone and the settings you choose (for example sleep hours, preferred currency, how you live).
  • What you create: notes, lists, reminders, events, activities, trips and travel documents, occasions, memories and photos, letters, check-ins, gestures, places and counters.
  • Your device for notifications: a push token so we can deliver notifications to your phone.
  • Messages to support: what you write to us, your email for the reply, and technical details only when you allow it.
  • Error reports: when the app hits an error it sends the error message, app version and platform — never your content. They are deleted after 60 days.

We do not collect your location, contacts, call logs, browsing or advertising identifiers. If you choose a contact or a calendar on your phone, that happens on your phone; only what you save in OneSpace is stored.

Who can see it

Everything in your space is visible only to you and your partner. Each item is either Shared (both of you) or Only me (just you). These rules are enforced by our database for every item, not only by the app. We do not access your content except to keep the service running, to answer a request you make, or when the law requires it.

Services we use

  • Supabase — our database, sign-in and file storage (hosted in the Asia-Pacific region).
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — to deliver notifications to your phone.
  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles for “Our map”, and place search: when you look up a place, its name, city and country are sent to OpenStreetMap’s search service.
  • Google or Apple sign-in — only if you choose to sign in that way.
  • Weather and public-holiday services — receive only a city or a country, never who you are.

We never sell your data or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account there is a 7-day period to change your mind; after that, your private items, files, devices and settings are deleted, and shared items stay with your partner with you shown as “Former partner”. Error reports are deleted after 60 days.

Your rights and choices

  • Download everything you created: Me → Your data and account → Download my data.
  • Correct your details in Me → Profile.
  • Delete your account in the app, or ask us on the Delete your account page.
  • Notifications can be turned off at any time in the app or in your phone settings.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access is checked on the server for every item. On your phone you can lock the app with your fingerprint or phone PIN. No system is perfect; if something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell you.

Children

OneSpace is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
