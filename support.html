How can we help?

Ask a question, report a problem, share an idea or ask to join the test. You don't need to be signed in. We reply by email.

We only use your email to reply. See the privacy policy.

Quick answers

I can't sign in

Use “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in screen and open the email on the same phone. If you signed up with Google or Apple, use the same button again. Still stuck? Send the form above with “I can't sign in”.

I didn't get the confirmation email

Look in Spam or Promotions, then tap “Resend confirmation” on the sign-in screen.

How do I invite my partner?

In the app, create your space and tap Invite. Send the link by WhatsApp, SMS or email — or let your partner scan the QR code from your screen.

How do I install the Android test version?

Open the file we send you and allow “install unknown apps” when your phone asks. For updates, install the new file over the old one — never uninstall first, so nothing is lost.
