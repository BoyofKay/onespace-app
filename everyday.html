Everything a home runs on, in one place both of you can see: lists with owners and repeats, reminders that know who they are for, one calendar, notes worth keeping and the contacts you always need.

How OneSpace solves it

Four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Lists

    Groceries, chores, packing — assigned, repeating, skipping holidays and days off.

  2. 2

    Reminders

    For me, my partner or both; repeat rules; snooze; linked to a to-do, trip, event or note.

  3. 3

    Calendar

    Month, week and day views, public holidays, days off — sent one way to your phone calendar.

  4. 4

    Contacts & notes

    Import from your phone, save back to it, spot duplicates; rich notes with photos and links.

Will it sync with Google Calendar?

OneSpace sends your shared events one way to your phone calendar (Google, Samsung or iPhone). It never reads your other calendars.
