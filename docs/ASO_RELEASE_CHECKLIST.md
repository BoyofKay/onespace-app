# OneSpace ASO readiness — material we can prepare without store account access
Date: 2026-11-18. The editable copy is in `docs/ASO_PRELAUNCH.md`. **Not submitted to Apple or Google.**

## Policy checks completed
- App name `OneSpace` meets both stores' 30-character limit.
- English Google Play short description <80 characters; full description <4,000 characters. Arabic short description likewise <80 characters.
- Apple name/subtitle <30 characters; keyword fields <=100 characters; avoid duplicates, competitor names, unsupported features, discounts and ranking claims.
- The mobile app supports **English and Arabic**, not the website's other three languages.
- No imaginary ratings, testimonials, downloads, rankings, subscriptions, guarantee of free lifetime access or fake store badges.
- Do not claim iOS native Share Extension or unreleased Apple device features.
- Screenshots must be from working build; Apple screenshots must be captured on actual iOS/TestFlight, not resized Android imagery.
