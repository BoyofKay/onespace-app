How OneSpace solves it

Four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Save a memory

    A story, a collage or a daily selfie — with place, date and captions.

  2. 2

    Pick a page design

    22+ designs: Our First Date, Wedding Day, Miles Apart, Date Night, Birthday Bash, Film Strip, Our Year…

  3. 3

    Fill it together

    Both of you can add photos and words to any page of a shared scrapbook — and Nudge partner asks for their part.

  4. 4

    Keep it

    Save or share as a PDF — print it, email it, keep it.

Everything it can do.

Daily selfie, together

Scrapbook studio

Type and tap right on the page; remove pages; read page by page.

9 collage layouts

Tags
