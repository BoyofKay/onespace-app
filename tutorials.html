YOUR FIRST SIX STEPS

One small thing at a time.

Based on the current OneSpace navigation and workflows. Demo content is illustrative.

STEP 01

Begin in Today

See what needs your attention and what the next seven days hold.

STEP 02

Capture something

On Android, share a link into OneSpace's Items to sort, or add something directly in Remember.

STEP 03

Give it a home

Turn an idea into a note, a to-do, an activity or another useful item.

STEP 04

Decide together

Use a Coordinate activity to discuss options, compare them and save the decision.

STEP 05

Keep the moment

Save a memory or send a thoughtful gesture manually through Connect.

STEP 06

Make it yours

Set your language, theme and notification preferences under Me.
