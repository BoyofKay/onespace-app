Getting started

What is OneSpace?

Your private space for everyday life: calendar, to-do lists, reminders, notes, trips, occasions and memories in one place.

Use it on your own, or invite your partner. Together you also get letters, daily check-ins, little gestures and shared plans, whether you live together or far apart.

Can I use OneSpace without a partner?

Yes. OneSpace works fully on your own. Everything you add stays yours.

When someone special comes along, you can invite them. Your “Only me” items stay private even then.

How do I get OneSpace, and is there an iPhone app?

OneSpace is in closed testing on Android through Google Play. Register on onespace.live to receive an invitation.

The iPhone version is being prepared and will first be shared through TestFlight. Your account and your space are the same on both phones.

Is OneSpace free?

Yes, during the early test. If that ever changes, we will tell you clearly and in advance.

How do I invite my partner?
  1. On Today, tap Invite my partner.
  2. Enter your partner’s email address and send.
  3. Your partner opens the email (or uses the code or QR code) and accepts within 48 hours.

Only the account with that verified email can join. Your “Only me” items stay private.

How do I resend, replace or revoke an invitation?

On Today, tap Manage invitation.

  • Resend email: same code, same expiry.
  • Generate a replacement code: the old code stops working at once.
  • Revoke invitation: nobody can use it any more.
I confirmed my email but cannot find my invitation. What should I do?

Sign in using the invited email. OneSpace checks for a pending invitation even if the browser did not pass its link. Retry the lookup if needed; do not create another shared space while trying to join.

Do we each need an account?

Yes. Each of you signs in with your own email. You share one space for “Shared” items, and each keeps a private side for “Only me” items.

Does it work whether we live together or apart?

Yes. Once your partner has joined, set how you live in either place:

  • Me → Settings → Our relationship → Living arrangement & meeting dates (also your last visit and next reunion).
  • On Today, tap the home or airplane icon and choose Together or Apart.

It is one shared setting, and your partner is notified when it changes. Apart is split automatically into the same or different time zones from your two phones. It does not apply while you use OneSpace on your own.

What is on each tab?
  • Today: what needs you now, what is coming up, and updates since you were away.
  • Remember: calendar, to-do lists, reminders, notes, counters, occasions, places and contact cards.
  • Coordinate: Activities (plans and decisions) and trips.
  • Connect: letters, a little love, daily check-ins, shared experiences and memories.
  • Me: profile, settings, help and your data.
Do we need to be online at the same time?

No. Each of you adds things when it suits you. Shared changes appear for the other partner the next time they open the app.

This is a test version. What should I report?

Anything that breaks, looks wrong, is confusing or is missing.

  • Problems: Me → Report a technical issue (you can add a screenshot).
  • Ideas and questions: Me → Contact us.

Errors are also sent to us automatically, without any of your content.

How to use each feature

How do I use Today?

Today is your start page: what needs you now, what is coming up and what changed while you were away.

  • Clocks and weather: when you live apart, each of you has a column with your local time and the weather of the city you chose (temperature, high and low, chance of rain). Choose your city in Me → Settings. When you live together, the card shows one home instead.
  • Good time to talk: when you live in different time zones, Today shows when you are both awake and free, based on your work and sleep hours.
  • The home or airplane icon shows how you live; tap it to change between Together and Apart (your partner is told).
  • Needs attention lists overdue and urgent items, reminders due now and check-ins waiting for you. Tap one to open it.
  • Coming up shows the next dates, trips and occasions. Since you were away lists your partner’s updates.
  • Quick add saves a reminder, to-do, note, place or trip in two taps, and the shortcuts open the parts you use most.

On your own, Today shows your clock, weather, plans and reminders without the partner parts.

Step-by-step tutorial →

How do I use the calendar?
  1. Go to Remember → Calendar and tap +.
  2. Write what is happening and set the date and time (or All day). Add a place if you like.
  3. Choose who it is for: me, my partner or both of us.
  4. Choose who can see it: Shared or Only me. Then save.
  • The day view has one lane for each of you. An event for one of you appears in that person’s lane; an event for both of you spans both lanes. Your work and sleep hours are shaded, so you can see when you are both free.
  • A shared event shows on your partner’s calendar in their own time zone.
  • From an event you can create a reminder, a to-do or a note, or open its place in Maps.

On your own, events are Only me and for you, and the day view shows one lane.

Step-by-step tutorial →

How do I use to-do lists and checklists?
  1. Go to Remember → To-do and tap +.
  2. Write a title. To make a checklist, type one item per line: each line becomes its own item to tick.
  3. Optional: set the priority, a date, who it belongs to, and Shared or Only me.
  4. For a routine, choose Repeat. Each time it starts fresh, and it can skip the days off of the person it belongs to.

A shared list updates on both phones as items are ticked.

Step-by-step tutorial →

How do I use reminders?
  1. Go to Remember → Reminders and tap +.
  2. Write what to remember and when. Choose who it is for: you, your partner or both.
  3. Make it repeat if needed. Mark it Urgent only if it must ring during sleep hours.

When it rings, mark it done, snooze it or add a follow-up note. Type @ in the details to mention your partner.

Step-by-step tutorial →

How do I use notes?
  1. Go to Remember → Notes and tap +.
  2. Write and format your note. Type @ to mention your partner; they get a notification.
  3. Choose Shared or Only me, then save. From a note you can also create a reminder, an event or a to-do.

Step-by-step tutorial →

How do I use Activities to plan and decide together?

An Activity holds one plan or decision from start to finish: the options, the conversation and the outcome.

  1. Go to Coordinate → New Activity and choose what it is for: buy something, find a service or provider, plan an event, decide something, or just a note.
  2. Set the priority (low to urgent), the status (exploring or comparing), a due date and Shared or Only me.
  3. Add options and details: products or links with prices, specifications, steps, photos, map locations, voice notes and contact information.
  4. Talk it through in the Conversation and react to each option with Like or Dislike.
  5. Tap Compare to see the options side by side, choose one, and record the outcome (for example Purchased, Booked or signed up, or Done or received).
  • Compare shows one column per option and one row per detail (price, specifications, pros and cons). An empty cell means not recorded yet.
  • Link the Activity to a trip, occasion, experience, event, reminder, to-do, note or memory, or use Create from this to make a reminder, an event, a to-do, a note or a trip from it.
  • A chosen gift option can be sent to an occasion’s shopping plan.
  • Contact information you attach is also saved as a card in Contact cards, with the same privacy as the Activity.
  • From another app or website, tap Share → OneSpace and choose Activity to add a link as a new Activity or as an option.

On your own, Activities work the same way as a private planner; the conversation and reactions are just yours.

Step-by-step tutorial →

How do I plan a trip?
  1. Go to Coordinate → Trips and tap +. Give it a name, the type of trip and where and when (dates can wait). Choose Both of us, or Only me for a surprise.
  2. Flights: tap Search on Google Flights (it opens with your airports, dates and travellers). When you find one, share it back to OneSpace or copy the link and tap Paste a flight.
  3. Compare the saved flight options (the price you saw, baggage, seats, pros and cons), then add the one you book to the itinerary. Its dates fill in the trip.
  4. Build the itinerary with stays, activities and reservations, in local times.
  5. Add a travel checklist and attach travel documents (boarding passes, tickets, visas; PDF or photo up to 10 MB). Only people who can see the trip can open them.
  6. Before you leave, use Create from this for a leave-for-the-airport reminder (6:00 on the day) or an online check-in reminder (9:00 the day before).
  • Each trip has its own conversation, and Costs so far adds up what you entered.
  • Booking sites and travel links shared into OneSpace can go straight to a trip.
  • Nothing is bought or checked in for you: prices are what you saw when saving.

Step-by-step tutorial →

How do I prepare for an occasion?
  1. Go to Remember → Occasions and tap +. Pick one of your saved special dates or a public holiday, or type your own (a birthday, anniversary, graduation…). Choose who it is for and Shared or Only me.
  2. Set a total budget. The budget shows what is reserved, what is spent and what is left, and warns you when you go over.
  3. Add gift options with a product link, price, shop, rating and expected delivery. Compare lists of gifts and try different combinations before you decide.
  4. Reserve your choices (the cost is set aside), then mark each as purchased when you order it (the cost moves to Spent). Track the order number, status and delivery; you are warned if it arrives after the date.
  5. Set preparation reminders: save money, research choices, order in time, final preparations. Nothing is scheduled until you choose.
  • Planning intelligence gives tips such as nothing reserved yet with the date close, items without a price, or a budget overrun.
  • Shop links shared into OneSpace can become gift ideas, and an option chosen in an Activity can be sent to the occasion.
  • Planning a surprise? Make it Only me: your partner sees nothing until you share it.

Tip: save birthdays and your anniversary once in Me → Settings → Birthdays, anniversary & special dates, and they are ready to pick here.

Step-by-step tutorial →

How do I use counters and timelines?

A counter shows how long since or until a moment that matters: days, months and years, with the next milestone.

  1. Go to Remember → Counters and tap +. Write what happened or is coming, and the date. Or pick it from your calendar, special dates, occasions, flights or trips.
  2. Choose Every year for anniversaries and birthdays, and Shared or Only me.
  3. To see the time between moments, tap Timelines, create a named timeline (Shared or Only me) and add counters to it. One counter can be on several timelines.

A timeline shows the time between each pair of moments, and Today appears on it when today falls between the first and last. On your own, counters and timelines are Only me.

Step-by-step tutorial →

How do I use Our places and the map?
  1. Go to Remember → Our places and tap +. Search the map, paste a Google Maps link, or tap the map where the place is (or press and hold).
  2. Choose Been here (a visit, with the date and your rating) or Add to bucket list (a place you want to go). Choose Shared or Only me.
  • On the map, a red check pin is a place you have been, a teal bookmark pin is on your bucket list, and each pin shows a category icon. The initials on a pin show who can see it.
  • From a bucket-list place, tap Plan a trip to start a trip there; the place then shows In a planned trip.
  • Photos and stories of a visit live in Memories. In Google Maps you can also tap Share → OneSpace to save a place.

Step-by-step tutorial →

How do I use contact cards?
  1. Go to Remember → Contact cards and tap +, or tap Import from phone to start from one of your phone contacts.
  2. Add the name on the card, a contact person, phone, email, website, address, opening hours, work details and notes.
  3. Choose Shared or Only me and save.
  • From a card you can call, email, open the website or the address in Maps, or tap Save to phone to add it to your phone contacts.
  • Contacts you add inside an Activity also appear here, with the Activity’s privacy.
  • OneSpace only stores the cards you save; it never uploads your phone contacts.

Step-by-step tutorial →

How do I use letters?
  1. Go to Connect → Letters and start a new letter.
  2. Write it. Save privately as a draft as often as you like; only you see drafts.
  3. Optional: choose Open on a special day. Your partner can open it from that date.
  4. Send it. A sent letter cannot be edited and stays in your shared history. You see when your partner has read it, and they can write a reply.

On your own, you can write and keep private drafts, and send them once you connect a partner.

Step-by-step tutorial →

How do I use A little love?
  1. Go to Connect → A little love and tap a gesture: a hug, thinking of you, good morning, good night, thank you, proud of you, I miss you and more.
  2. OneSpace picks a message for it at random from that gesture’s pool. Edit the message before sending if you want your own words.
  3. Send it. Your partner gets a notification, and both of you see it in the sent and received history.
  • Make it yours: tap Create my own gesture for a gesture with your own name and message. In Me → Settings → Love gesture settings you can edit or hide the built-in messages, add your own, and mark one Don’t pick this one at random.
  • Living together, the “from miles away” messages and the missing-home gestures are left out. Living apart, you also get gestures such as I miss us together and missing our home.

A little love needs a partner. On your own it is not available.

How do I use daily check-ins?
  • Feelings journal: go to Connect → Daily check-ins and record how you feel today, once a day. You can edit today’s feeling, and search your journal later.
  • With a partner, you can share your feeling with them, or ask them how they are. They reply when they choose, and you can answer back with a gesture.
  • Only your partner sees a feeling you share with them.

On your own, the feelings journal works fully as your private diary.

Step-by-step tutorial →

How do I use shared experiences?
  1. Go to Connect → Shared experiences and pick a category: movies, shows, music, books or games. Each tile shows how many are still to enjoy.
  2. Add one with its title, where to watch, read or play, and a link. You can also share it from Netflix, Spotify or any website with Share → OneSpace.
  3. Each of you marks Interested on your own. When you both do, it becomes a mutual pick.
  4. Tap Plan a date to choose when you will enjoy it together; your partner sees the plan. Open link takes you straight to the platform.
  5. Afterwards, mark it enjoyed, then rate and review it. Talk about it in the comments.

Now and then, OneSpace suggests “What shall we do together?” from your list, preferring mutual picks. On your own, it works as your personal watch, read and play list.

Step-by-step tutorial →

How do I keep memories, make a scrapbook and take daily selfies?
  1. Go to Connect → Memories and tap +. Add a title, date, place, the story and photos. People and photo tags make it easy to find later.
  2. For a scrapbook, pick a page design and fill it right on the page. Add as many pages as you like with New page.
  3. In a shared scrapbook you can both edit every page. Use Nudge partner in the memory menu to ask for their part.
  4. Save or share the finished scrapbook as a PDF (saved in Downloads › OneSpace).
  • Daily selfie: one selfie a day. In a couple it is made together: each of you adds your own photo to the same day (Add yours to today’s selfie).
  • The selfie calendar shows your days with a selfie; a small photo in the corner is your partner’s.

On your own, memories, scrapbooks and daily selfies are your private story.

How do I save something from another app or website?
  1. In any app or website, tap Share and choose OneSpace (Android).
  2. OneSpace suggests up to three places for it, based on what you shared: a shop link → gift idea, Activity option or to-do; a movie or song → shared experience; a flight → flight option or trip; a Google Maps place → bucket list or visited place; text with a date → reminder, to-do or event. More… shows every other choice.
  3. Not sure yet? Save it to Items to sort (only you see it) and sort it later; items older than 30 days get a gentle “still want this?” label.

Privacy and sharing

Who can see what I add?

Only you, and the partner you invite. Nobody else.

  • Shared: you both see it, can edit it and may get a notification.
  • Only me: only you see it, never a current or future partner.
  • If you separate: everything Shared is permanently deleted for both of you; each of you keeps your own Only me items.

Each item shows Shared or Only me at the bottom of its page.

Can I plan a surprise?

Yes. Make the occasion, trip, gift idea or note Only me. Your partner sees nothing and gets no notification until you choose to share it.

Does OneSpace track my location or my phone?

No. There is no location tracking, no activity tracking and no “online” status. For weather you choose a city yourself.

Do you sell data or show ads?

No. We do not sell data, show ads or read your content to profile you.

Does OneSpace upload all my contacts?

No. You pick one contact on your phone, and only the card you save is stored in OneSpace.

Can a new partner see an old relationship’s content?

No. Shared content is deleted when a shared space ends, and your Only me items are never shown to any partner.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. OneSpace can ask for your phone PIN, fingerprint or face unlock when it opens.

Can support see the screenshot I attach to a ticket?

Yes, only that screenshot, and only to help with your ticket. Each view is logged. Support never sees your notes, photos or messages.

Notifications and settings

I am not getting notifications. What should I check?
  1. Allow notifications for OneSpace in your phone settings.
  2. Make sure battery saving is not restricting OneSpace.
  3. Remember that alerts wait during sleep hours unless they are Urgent.

Still nothing? Use Me → Report a technical issue.

How do sleep hours and time zones work?

Each of you sets work and sleep hours in Me → Settings → Schedule & days off. Times show in each phone’s own time zone.

Non-urgent alerts are held until the other person wakes up.

How do I change the language or currency?

Me → Language and currency. Each of you chooses English or Arabic and your preferred currency.

The website is also available in Spanish, French and German.

Can I change the colors and background?

Yes. Me → Settings → Appearance & themes: choose a color theme, light or dark, and a background.

Can OneSpace show in my phone calendar?

Yes: Me → Settings → Show in my phone calendar. Your events, trips, occasions and special dates appear in a separate “OneSpace” calendar.

It works one way: OneSpace never reads your other calendars.

Does OneSpace work on a tablet or in landscape?

OneSpace is designed for phones and stays in portrait. It runs on supported tablets in portrait too.

Account, data and separation

How do I download my data?

Me → Your data and account → Download my data. Your account information is saved as a JSON file on your phone.

Photos, documents and other attachments are not included. Keep the file private.

What happens if I delete my account?
  1. For 7 days nothing is deleted, and you can cancel at any time.
  2. After that, your account, your Only me items, photos and settings are permanently deleted.
  3. What you share with your current partner is deleted too. Your partner keeps their own Only me items.
What happens if we separate?

If either of you leaves the shared space, removes the other or deletes it, everything shared in it is permanently deleted for both of you.

There is no archive or backup of shared content. Each of you keeps your own Only me items and can invite someone new later.

Where did something I removed go?

Most lists have a Removed filter where you can find and restore items you removed one by one. Where a 30-day period applies, a removed item can be restored for 30 days and is then cleared for good.

This does not apply to deleting a shared space or an account: that cannot be undone.

I changed phones. Will our space disappear?

No. Your space is stored with your account. Install OneSpace on the new phone and sign in with the same email.

What is the Founder badge?

The first 500 people to join OneSpace are Founder Members. The badge appears on your profile in Me.

Signing in and help

I cannot sign in.
  1. Check the email address for typos.
  2. Tap Forgot your password? and open the link on the same phone.
  3. Still stuck? Tap Contact us at the bottom of the sign-in screen and choose I cannot sign in. No sign-in is needed; we reply by email.
I did not get the confirmation email.
  1. Look in Spam or Promotions.
  2. On the sign-in screen, tap Resend confirmation email.
  3. Open the link on the same phone so it returns you to OneSpace.
How do I report a problem or send an idea?
  • A problem: Me → Report a technical issue. Describe what happened and add a screenshot if you can.
  • An idea, question or complaint: Me → Contact us.

No account or not signed in? Tap Contact us at the bottom of the sign-in screen, or use the support page on onespace.live. We reply by email. Your notes, photos and messages are never included.

Something does not show up on my partner’s phone. What should I do?
  1. Check that the item is Shared, not Only me.
  2. Both phones need an internet connection. Pull down to refresh the list.
  3. Still missing? Use Me → Report a technical issue.

Founder Members

What is the Founding 500?
The first 500 people who join and confirm their email — on their own or as a couple — become Founder Members. You get the app first, a direct line to the team and a say in what we build.
Do we both need to sign up?
No. You can register on your own. If you want to invite your partner to the Founder program, send them the link from your confirmation page. They must register and confirm their own email; Founder status follows available places and the order of confirmation.
When does my Founder membership start?
As soon as you click the link in the confirmation email from founders@onespace.live. Places go in the order emails are confirmed.
The confirmation email didn’t arrive.
Check spam or Promotions for founders@onespace.live. Still nothing after 10 minutes? Sign up again with the same email and we will send a fresh link.
What do Founder Members get?
Early access, a direct line to the team and a say in what we build. Founder benefits will be announced before any pricing is introduced.
Can I stop the emails?
Yes — every email has an Unsubscribe link, and you stay a Founder Member.
Can iPhone owners register today?
Yes. Founding 500 registration is open to Android and iPhone owners. We invite each member when their platform is ready.
Is Founder status for one person or the couple?
It belongs to each person. You can join on your own or as a couple, and a partner who joins with your invitation becomes a Founder Member too.
What if all Founder places are taken?
The first 500 people who confirm their email become Founder Members. Later signups can join the waiting list for future invitations.
Does signing up make me a Founder Member immediately?
Not until you confirm your email. Founder status follows the real order of confirmation, and there are no Founder numbers.
Do Founder registrations include a guaranteed lifetime price?
No lifetime price or subscription benefit has been promised. Founder perks and any future pricing will be explained before they take effect.
