One list per kind

Every app keeps its own list. OneSpace keeps one for both of you.

Five streaming services, four shops, three flight sites — each with its own list, and each of you with your own account. That is easily 20 lists between two people. Tap Share in any of them and OneSpace puts it in the one list where it belongs.

How OneSpace solves it

Four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Add from anywhere

    Share from a streaming or music app, or add a title by hand.

  2. 2

    Each of you picks

    Interested or not — independently. Both interested = a mutual pick.

  3. 3

    Plan a date

    Turn a pick into a movie night on your calendar.

  4. 4

    Rate and remember

    Mark watched, listened, read or played — rate and review, separately.
