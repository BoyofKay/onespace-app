Birthdays and anniversaries, planned weeks ahead — with a real budget

OneSpace remembers the date, reminds you to start in time, collects gift ideas from any shop, compares them, tracks the budget and warns you if a delivery would arrive late. Mark it “Only me” and it stays a surprise.

How OneSpace solves it

Four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Pick the occasion

    From your special dates (birthdays, anniversary), national holidays, or type your own.

  2. 2

    Collect gift ideas

    Share products from any shop, or bring options you already compared in an Activity.

  3. 3

    Compare and plan the budget

    Try different combinations, save proposed gift lists, see what is left after every choice.

  4. 4

    Order, track, celebrate

    Order number, tracking link, expected delivery — and a reminder to start preparing.

Occasion budget

Gift options

Price, shop, rating, how much you like it, notes and the link — wish, shortlisted, planned, purchased.
