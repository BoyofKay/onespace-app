Delete your OneSpace account

You can delete your account at any time, from the app or from this page.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open OneSpace and go to Me.
  2. Tap Your data and account.
  3. Optional: tap Download my data to keep a copy.
  4. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • You have 7 days to change your mind: sign in again and choose “Keep my account”.
  • After 7 days we delete your private items and their files, your devices and notification tokens, your personal settings and your membership of the shared space. Your sign-in is removed (your email is anonymised).
  • Shared items stay with your partner, shown as written by “Former partner”, because they belong to both of you.
  • Messages you sent to support are kept so we can answer you — ask us and we will delete them too. Error reports (no content) are deleted after 60 days.

Can't open the app?

Send this form from the email address of your OneSpace account. We will confirm by email before deleting anything, then follow the same steps as above.

We only use your email to reply. See the privacy policy.
