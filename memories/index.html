How OneSpace solves it

Four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Save a memory

    A story, a collage or a daily selfie — with place, date and captions.

  2. 2

    Pick a page design

    22+ designs: Our First Date, Wedding Day, Miles Apart, Date Night, Birthday Bash, Film Strip, Our Year…

  3. 3

    Fill it together

    Invite your partner to add photos and words to any page.

  4. 4

    Keep it

    Save or share as a PDF — print it, email it, keep it.

In detail

Everything it can do.

🤳

Daily selfie, together

Each of you adds your own photo to the same day — a diary of your life, or of the distance.

📒

Scrapbook studio

Type and tap right on the page; remove pages; read page by page.

🖼

9 collage layouts

Grid, feature, polaroid, overlap, carousel, mosaic, film, scatter, timeline.

🏷

Tags

Who is in the photo, where it was, and anything else.

📄

PDF in one tap

Private storage
