One list per kind

Every app keeps its own list. OneSpace keeps one for both of you.

Five streaming services, four shops, three flight sites — each with its own list, and each of you with your own account. That is easily 20 lists between two people. Tap Share in any of them and OneSpace puts it in the one list where it belongs.

How OneSpace solves it

Three simple steps.

  1. 1

    Share from the app you’re in

    Amazon, eBay, IKEA, Etsy, Netflix, Spotify, YouTube, Google Flights, Skyscanner, Booking.com, Google Maps, Waze — or any web page, text, photo or PDF.

  2. 2

    OneSpace suggests the best place

    It recognises the content itself — a shop, a film, a flight, a map, a date — and offers its top three destinations.

  3. 3

    Save — or sort it later

    Not sure yet? It waits privately in “Items to sort” until you decide.

Does OneSpace read my other apps?

No. It only sees what you choose to share with it, and nothing is saved until you tap Save.

Does it work on iPhone?

Sharing into OneSpace works on Android today. On iPhone it is coming with the iPhone release.
