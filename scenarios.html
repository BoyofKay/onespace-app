Six moments from couples who live in different places.

Living together

Seven moments from couples who share the same home.

Sunday morning, no one has to remember everythingA quiet start instead of a hundred little questions.+
Scene 01 · The moment

INT. KITCHEN, MORNING. Lina: “Did we buy coffee?” Omar: “And who's sorting the laundry?” Both reach for their phones.

Scene 02 · The turning point

CUT TO: REMEMBER. They open one shared checklist, tick what is done and assign the remaining chores. A repeating reminder covers the bins.

Scene 03 · The payoff

EXT. NEIGHBORHOOD CAFÉ. Their chores are visible to both; now they can enjoy breakfast without being the household's human reminder.

OneSpace functions in this episode Shared checklists · Assigned tasks · Repeating reminders Explore the features
The sofa debate, finally settledTwo tastes, three shops and one decision.+
Scene 01 · The moment

INT. LIVING ROOM. Omar: “The blue one is perfect.” Lina: “Perfect for a different living room.” Silence.

Scene 02 · The turning point

CUT TO: COORDINATE. They save links, prices, dimensions and both opinions in one activity. Their preferred sofa emerges from the comparison.

Scene 03 · The payoff

INT. LIVING ROOM, TWO WEEKS LATER. They sit together on the sofa they both chose. The final decision stays saved.

A birthday surprise without spoiling itPrivate planning can still be thoughtful planning.+
Scene 01 · The moment
