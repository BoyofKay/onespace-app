Today

Open the app, know what matters.

Today brings together what your partner sent, what needs your answer, and the next seven days — shaped by whether you live together or apart.

  • Since you were away — gestures, letters and updates you missed.
  • Needs your attention — votes, decisions and items assigned to you.
  • Two clocks & weather — when you live apart, see both cities at once.
  • Quick add — a reminder, to-do, note, place or trip in two taps.
  • Your shortcuts — pin the screens you use most.

Remember

Your shared memory, organised.

Counters for every special date, your places on a map, notes, contact cards and memories that become scrapbooks.

  • Counters — days, weeks, months and years — the date itself and its anniversary, with progress bars.
  • Linked dates — see the time between two moments, like first date → wedding.
  • Our places — been there and bucket list, pinned on a map, shared from Maps.
  • Memories & scrapbooks — stories, daily selfies of both of you, save as PDF.
  • Notes & contact cards — rich notes and the details you always need — sizes, the doctor, the plumber.

Coordinate

Decide together, without the back-and-forth.

A shared calendar, lists and reminders — plus activities where you each mark the options you like and decide together.

  • Calendar — one calendar for both of you, sent one way to your phone calendar if you like.
  • To-do lists & reminders — assign to one of you or both, with due dates and repeats.
  • Activities — compare options, vote, talk it through with voice notes and @mentions, then decide.

Connect

Small, daily ways to feel close.

A little love in one tap, letters that wait for the right day, and a daily check-in that takes less than a minute.

  • A little love — a hug, a kiss or kind words in one tap — with your own message or a built-in one.
  • Letters — sealed until a date, with a countdown.
  • Daily connection & experiences — feelings, a journal, and films, books or places you enjoy together.
Me

Set it up your way.

Nine themes, Arabic or English, notifications grouped by day, privacy controls and a full export.

  • Notifications that explain themselves — who, what and when, grouped by day.
  • Help without signing in — contact us even if you are locked out.
