How OneSpace solves it

Four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Start an Activity

    Give it a title, a date if there is one, and who it belongs to.

  2. 2

    Add what you find

    Options, links, prices, photos, notes, steps — share them straight in from any app.

  3. 3

    Talk and pick

    Each of you marks what you like. Chat right there with @mentions and voice notes.

  4. 4

    Decide and follow up

    Record the decision and outcome, then add a reminder or event — they stay linked.
