# OneSpace founder launch — four-campaign plan
Draft date: 2026-11-18. **PLANNING ONLY. Nothing approved, scheduled, published or bought.**

## Single conversion goal
Get qualified couples to join and **confirm** the Founding 500 waitlist (one shared Founder number per couple; the first 250 confirmed couples = 500 people). Registration is open to owners of Android **and iPhone** now; the app invitation is platform-dependent. Do not promise a release date, perpetual free access or lifetime discounts.

## Campaigns, concept and creative
| Campaign | Audience | Message / proof | Creative | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One Life, Two Places | Long-distance couples across time zones | Couples can share practical life while apart: plans, trip documents, counters by choice, gestures and updates | 30-second real Android walkthrough + an illustrated fictional reunion journey, explicitly labeled illustrative | Join the Founding 500 |
| Same Home, Shared Load | Couples living together | Shared routines, recurring checklists, decisions and reminders reduce forgotten responsibilities | Before/after household Sunday reset using authentic app UI | Join the Founding 500 |
| From a Shared Link to a Shared Decision | Couples planning purchases, trips or events | A link, photo or location can lead to a shared comparison and decision in one place | Three-panel Android-supported smart sharing into Coordinate; no iPhone share extension claim | See the demo → Join |
| Every Reunion Has a Story | Long-distance couples preparing a visit | Flight comparisons, checklists, travel documents and a memory when the visit ends | A 4-beat carousel with characters Lina & Omar labeled fictional | Read the journey → Join |

## Four-week organic pilot (subject to approval)
- **Week 1 — explain:** 2 posts about the problem, 1 short demo, 1 founder invitation. A/B test the two audience messages.
- **Week 2 — show:** 2 scenario carousels, 1 real product clip, 1 founder FAQ post. Lead with actual workflows.
- **Week 3 — engage:** 1 voluntary feedback poll, 2 micro tutorials, 1 screenshot carousel. Invite constructive comments, not reviews or ratings.
- **Week 4 — learn:** 2 highest-performing creatives adapted, 1 honest progress update, 1 founder invitation. Compare conversion, not vanity reach.
Maximum **4 posts/week per owned channel**, cross-post thoughtfully with each platform's norms. No automatic mass messaging, no purchased follows or group spam.

## Channels and permission
- Owned website, a product social account, relevant voluntary test communities and indie-launch directories.
- WhatsApp / Telegram / Facebook communities: only share after group admin permission; disclose founder affiliation. Immigration support groups are especially sensitive: acknowledge hardship sincerely without exploiting visa uncertainty or implying app utility speeds official processing.
- Arabic and English first; other website languages are not evidence that the mobile app supports them.
- Do not impersonate users or frame fictional scenarios as testimonials.

## Measurement
UTMs e.g. `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=one-life-two-places&utm_content=reunion-card-a`; tag by platform. Review page views → founder form start → email submitted → email confirmed → couple invited → partner joined, and Android/iPhone shares. Target baselines **after** measuring first-week actuals; no fabricated conversion targets.

## Cost policy
Start at **$0 ad spend**. Produce static images and genuine device captures from existing project assets. Any paid social, influencer sponsorship, email send or external placement requires explicit owner approval; do not imply automatic access to social accounts.

## Required approval before launch
Owner reviews each headline, CTA, two-language copy, images, dates, group permissions and any cost. Legal/privacy notices and opt-in unsubscribe practices must be respected.
