Getting started

What is OneSpace?

OneSpace is a private shared space for two people. It brings together calendars, to-do lists, reminders, notes, trips, occasions, memories, letters and thoughtful gestures — whether you live together or apart.

How do I connect my partner?

Create your space, send the invitation from the welcome screen, and have your partner accept it after signing up.

What are the five tabs?

Today, Remember, Coordinate, Connect and Me. Today is your dashboard; Remember is for things to keep track of; Coordinate helps with plans and trips; Connect brings you closer; Me contains settings, privacy and help.

Privacy

Who can see my entries?

OneSpace supports Shared and Only me visibility. Your partner sees shared entries; private items and drafts are only for you.

Can I plan a surprise privately?

Yes. Make the occasion, gift idea or note Only me. Your partner won't see it or receive notifications about it until you share.

Does OneSpace track my location?

No. OneSpace does not track your live location, phone activity or online status. You can choose cities for the weather display.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. Depending on your device, OneSpace can ask for the phone PIN, fingerprint or Face ID to unlock.

Everyday use

Where do removed items go?

Many removed items are recoverable. Use the relevant screen's filters to show removed items and select Restore from the item menu, where available.

How can I save something from another app?

On Android, use Share and choose OneSpace. Items to sort lets you turn the incoming content into a useful OneSpace entry. iOS sharing into OneSpace is planned.

Can we use different languages?

Yes. Each partner can choose their app language independently. The app interface supports English and Arabic.

Can I change the appearance?

Yes. Under Me → Settings → Appearance & themes, choose a color collection and light/dark or seasonal appearance.

Reminders

How do quiet hours work?

Each partner can set work and sleep hours. Non-urgent alerts respect the recipient's sleep schedule; urgent alerts are treated differently.

Account

What happens if I delete my account?

You have a seven-day cancellation period. After that, your private items and settings are deleted; shared items remain with the other partner. Read the account-deletion policy for details.

How do I download my data?

Me → Your data and account → Download my data. The export includes data records; photos and documents remain in the app.

Availability

Is OneSpace available for iPhone?

The iPhone version is being prepared for testing through TestFlight. Availability will be announced when ready.

Is OneSpace free?

Pricing has not been announced. OneSpace is currently in early testing.

What does joining the Founder list mean?

You are registering interest in launch and testing updates. Founder Member recognition will be confirmed individually; the form does not assign a number or guarantee immediate access.
