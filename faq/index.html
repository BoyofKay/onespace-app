Getting started

What is OneSpace?
A private app for two people in a relationship: a shared calendar, lists, reminders, trips, occasions, letters, little gestures, counters, places and memories in one place.
How do I get it?
OneSpace is in an early test on Android. Join the Founding 500, confirm your email, and we will invite couples in small groups. iPhone is coming next.
How do I invite my partner?
After creating your space, share your invite code or its QR code. Your partner signs up and taps “Scan your partner’s QR code”. A space holds exactly two people.
Is it free?
Yes, during the early test. If that ever changes, we will tell you clearly and in advance.
Do we each need an account?
Yes. Each partner signs in with a separate account. An invitation connects both accounts to the same private space.
Can I join before my partner is ready?
Yes. Create your space and invite your partner later. Shared collaboration begins once you both join.
Can we change how we live without starting over?
Yes. Switch between living apart and living together. Today adapts without creating a new account or shared space.
Can we both register for Founder access before the iPhone app is ready?
Yes. Founder registration is open to Android and iPhone users now. Select your platform when signing up; an invitation arrives when its version is ready.
Do we need to be online at the same time to use OneSpace?
No. Each of you can add information, comments, plans or memories when convenient. Shared changes are available to the other partner when they open the app and sync.

Founder Members

What is the Founding 500?
The first 250 couples — 500 people — who join and confirm their email become Founder Members. You get the app first, a direct line to the team and a say in what we build.
Do we both need to sign up?
One of you is enough to claim a place. After confirming, you get an invite link for your partner — they join under the same Founder number.
When does our Founder number come?
When you click the link in the confirmation email from founders@onespace.live. Numbers go in the order emails are confirmed.
The confirmation email didn’t arrive.
Check spam or Promotions for founders@onespace.live. Still nothing after 10 minutes? Sign up again with the same email and we will send a fresh link.
What do Founder Members get?
Early access, a direct line to the team and a say in what we build. Founder benefits will be announced before any pricing is introduced.
Can I stop the emails?
Yes — every email has an Unsubscribe link, and your Founder number stays yours.
Can iPhone owners register today?
Yes. Founding 500 registration is open to Android and iPhone owners. We invite each member when their platform is ready.
Is our Founder number for one person or the couple?
It belongs to the couple. One confirmed couple shares one number, and the second partner joins using the invitation link.
What if all Founder places are taken?
The first 250 confirmed couples receive Founder numbers. Later signups can join the waiting list for future invitations.
Does signing up reserve a Founder number immediately?
Not until you confirm your email. Confirmed registrations are assigned in order; partners use one shared Founder couple number.
Do Founder registrations include a guaranteed lifetime price?
No lifetime price or subscription benefit has been promised. Founder perks and any future pricing will be explained before they take effect.

Using OneSpace

Does it work for couples who live together?
Yes. Choose “Together” and Today focuses on your home and shared plans. Distance-only messages are never sent.
Can I keep something from my partner?
Yes — mark items “Only me”, for example a surprise gift or occasion.
Does it sync with my phone calendar?
You can send OneSpace events one way to your phone calendar (Google, Samsung or iPhone calendar). Changes in your phone calendar do not come back.
Can I write my own love notes?
Yes. Every gesture can carry your own words, and you can set a private reminder to send a little love.
Which languages are supported?
English and Arabic (right to left). Each of you picks your own.
Do dates create counters automatically?
No. Counters are opt-in. You choose a date from a calendar event, occasion or trip, open the counter editor and save it yourself.
Can we both edit a shared plan?
Yes. Shared Coordinate activities can contain both partners' links, options, notes, comments and final decisions.
Does OneSpace read changes made in my phone calendar?
No. OneSpace currently sends its events one way to a separate phone calendar. Changes made in your phone calendar are not imported back.
Can I share from another iPhone app directly into OneSpace?
Not yet. The iOS share extension is still in development. Android supports share intake from compatible apps; links can also be added manually.
Do recurring checklists start fresh each time?
Yes. A new scheduled cycle has its own unchecked items, while earlier completions stay in their history. Yesterday’s ticks do not complete today’s chores.
Can an activity include photos and a location?
Yes. Add images, a location and relevant links or notes to the same shared activity, so both partners can evaluate the option in context.
Where should we keep tickets and documents for a trip?
Attach supported PDFs or images directly to the trip. Each document stays under the trip’s access permissions; remember to bring any legally required originals too.
Can both partners edit the same scrapbook?
Yes, when the scrapbook is shared and the invited partner has accepted the collaboration invitation. Both can then add pages, photos and text.
Can I save or share a complete scrapbook as a PDF?
The scrapbook viewer supports exporting the designed pages as one PDF and sharing it through your phone’s available sharing options. Availability should be confirmed on each released platform.
Will the app change its colors on our anniversary?
When an anniversary is saved and Seasonal appearance is set to Automatic, OneSpace can apply a romantic anniversary accent that day. Each partner’s appearance settings are personal.

Privacy & account

Who can see our space?
Only the two members. Photos and documents are stored privately and opened with short-lived links.
Do you sell data or show ads?
No, never.
How do I export or delete my data?
In the app: Me → Your data and account. Locked out? Use this form.
What happens if we break up?
Either of you can leave the space at any time from Me. You can export first.
What does Only me mean?
An Only me item remains private to its owner unless they explicitly grant supported view-only access. The server enforces access rules.
Does revoking access delete screenshots already taken?
No. Revocation blocks future authorized reads, but cannot remove a screenshot or copy someone has already saved.
Does OneSpace upload all my contacts?
No. You deliberately choose any phone contact you want to save. OneSpace does not automatically upload your address book.
Are private surprises included in our shared notifications?
Only-me items are private to their creator. Keep gifts, research and surprise plans private until you choose to reveal them; shared items remain visible according to their permissions.

Help

I can’t sign in.
Use “Forgot password” on the sign-in screen, or contact us — no account needed.
How do I report a bug?
In the app: Me → Help and support → Report a technical issue (it can add a screenshot and the details we need). Or use the website form.
Is there an iPhone app?
It is being prepared for TestFlight. Join early access and choose iPhone — we will invite you when it is ready.
Why does the website have more languages than the app?
The website has five languages. The current mobile app supports English and Arabic. Translated website content does not mean those languages are available in the app.
I changed phones. Will our space disappear?
No. Your space belongs to your accounts, not to one device. Sign in with your existing account and follow the device security checks.
What should I check if notifications stop arriving?
Check internet access, phone notification permissions and OneSpace preferences. iPhone push also needs completed Apple/Firebase configuration before testing. Contact Support if the problem continues.
What should I do if something does not sync?
Check your connection, reopen the item and avoid making the same edit simultaneously on both phones. If it persists, use Help or the website support form and mention the item type and approximate time.
