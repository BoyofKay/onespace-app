One list per kind

Every app keeps its own list. OneSpace keeps one for both of you.

Five streaming services, four shops, three flight sites — each with its own list, and each of you with your own account. That is easily 20 lists between two people. Tap Share in any of them and OneSpace puts it in the one list where it belongs.
