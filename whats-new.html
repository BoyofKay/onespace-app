Better every week, shaped by real couples.

OneSpace is in an early test. Here is what changed recently — and how to tell us what to build next.

November 2026

Made for you — on your own or together

  • Use OneSpace on your own, and invite someone when you’re ready.
  • Today shows your day, your partner’s and what you do together side by side — with work days, days off and public holidays for your country.
  • Events say who’s taking part and can have their own time zone.
  • A daily selfie calendar, scrapbooks you can both edit, and Nudge partner.
  • Invite your partner by email; invitations expire after 48 hours.
November 2026

The Founding 500 is open

  • Join with your email and confirm it to become a Founder Member.
  • Invite your partner with your own link — they become a Founder Member too.
  • The reunion counter is right in every “living apart” setting; theme previews fit smaller phones.
November 2026

Places, counters and a map

  • Our places: been there and bucket list, pinned on a map, shared from Google Maps.
  • Counters that tell the date itself from its anniversary, with progress bars and a Today line.
  • Plan a trip from the bucket-list places you pick.
November 2026

Clearer notifications and smarter gestures

  • Notifications now say who did what, grouped by day.
  • Love gestures in a logical order, and no “far away” messages for couples who live together.
  • @mentions built into the conversation field.
November 2026

Sign-up, help and calendars

  • Help, contact and “report a problem” from the sign-in screen and this website — no account needed.
  • Send OneSpace events to your phone calendar.
  • Arabic translation completed across Today, trips and check-ins.
