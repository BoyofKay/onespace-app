Delete your account and data

You are always in control. Here is exactly what happens.

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Version 1.1

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Me → Your data and account.
  2. Optional: Download my data first (photos and files are not included).
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

What is deleted

Nothing is deleted for 7 days — you can cancel at any time in that period and everything stays as it was. After 7 days, your account and your Only me items are permanently deleted, together with everything you shared with your current partner. Your partner keeps their own Only me items.

Can’t sign in?

Send a request below — or email privacy@onespace.live — from the email on your account. We will confirm it is you, then delete the account and reply.
