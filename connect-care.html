Home › Connect & Care

The heart of your relationship, with room for everything else.

Emotional connection is important. OneSpace combines it with planning, conversations, comparisons and decisions — so it supports the whole life you share.

LOVE LETTERS

Words worth waiting for.

Write a letter today and choose when your partner can open it, with a countdown until that moment.

  • Timed opening — for birthdays, anniversaries and special days.
  • Private message — the letter stays for its intended recipient.
A LITTLE LOVE

A small gesture can change a day.

Send a hug, a kiss, encouragement or your own words. A personal moment, not an automated ritual.

  • Your words — add a personal message to a gesture.
  • Shared history — look back at the gestures you have sent and received.
DAILY CHECK-INS

A little space to say how you really are.

Share how you are feeling and what is on your mind, even when you are busy or apart.

  • Daily reflection — take a moment to name how your day feels.
  • Share what matters — give your partner context beyond a quick reply.
FEELINGS JOURNAL

Give your feelings somewhere to live.

Record thoughts and moods over time. This section uses a general Connect screenshot until a dedicated journal capture is available.

  • Reflect over time — revisit your earlier notes and feelings.
  • Your context — keep what you want to remember without losing it in chat.

Emotional connection is only the beginning.

OneSpace also brings two-way editing, comparison, discussion and shared planning into the same space.
