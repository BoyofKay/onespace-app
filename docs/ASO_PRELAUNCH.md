## Google Play — English
- **App name (30 max):** OneSpace
- **Short description (80 max):** A private space for couples: shared plans, lists, love notes and memories.
- **Full description:**

One life. Two people. One shared space.

OneSpace helps couples keep everyday plans, conversations, little gestures and meaningful memories together — whether you live in different cities or share the same home.

Plan together. Create shared activities, compare options, discuss what matters and record the decisions you make as a couple.

Remember what matters. Organize a shared calendar, reminders, checklists, notes, important dates and contact details. Keep personal items private when you choose.

Explore together. Save places you've visited and places you want to go. Prepare trips with flight options, packing lists and travel details. Create counters for moments that matter — only when you choose to add them.

Stay connected. Send little love gestures, write letters, check in with each other and keep shared memories and scrapbooks.

Your space belongs to you two. Each partner has their own account, with shared and private items controlled by their permissions.

OneSpace supports English and Arabic in the mobile app. Some features and availability differ by platform while the iPhone app is being prepared.

Learn more and register for Founder invitations at https://onespace.live.
