GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things you might be wondering.

Is OneSpace only for long-distance couples?

Not at all. It adapts to couples living together, living apart in the same time zone, or living across time zones.

Can my partner see all my notes and plans?

No. Items can be Shared or Only me. Your private entries remain yours, while shared entries are available to your partner.

Is OneSpace available to download?

OneSpace is currently in early testing. Android testing is underway; iOS availability will be announced separately. Join early access for updates.

Do you sell our data or show ads?

No. The app's published privacy policy says it does not sell personal data or show ads. Read the full privacy policy for details about the information it handles.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. OneSpace includes data export and account-deletion controls. Shared content may remain with the other partner after one account is deleted, as explained in the account-deletion policy.
