Terms of use

The short version: OneSpace is a private space for you — and for you and your partner, if you invite one. Use it kindly and lawfully, keep your password safe, and remember that what you mark “Shared” is seen by your partner.

Your accountYour contentSharing with your partnerAcceptable useTest versionEndingLiabilityPrivacy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Version 1.1

Your account

You need to be at least 18. Give a real email address and keep your sign-in private. You are responsible for what happens under your account. You can use OneSpace on your own; a shared space connects exactly two people.

Your content

What you write and upload stays yours. You give OneSpace only the permission needed to store it, show it to you and your partner, and send you notifications about it. We do not sell it, show ads or use it to profile you.

Sharing with your partner

Items marked “Shared” are visible to your partner, who can also edit them, and may notify them. “Only me” items are visible only to you. If either of you leaves the shared space, removes the other or deletes it, everything shared in it is permanently deleted for both of you and cannot be restored; each person keeps their own “Only me” items. If you delete your account, the same happens after a 7-day period in which you can cancel.

Acceptable use

Do not use OneSpace to harass, threaten or spy on anyone, to store illegal material, or to break into other accounts or the service. We may suspend accounts that do.

Test version

OneSpace is in an early test with a small group of people. Features may change and something may break. Please keep your own copy of anything important and tell us about problems.

Ending

You can download your data and delete your account at any time (Me → Your data and account). We may end the test or the service with notice by email.

Liability

OneSpace is provided “as is”. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect losses or for missed reminders or notifications.

Privacy

How we handle data is explained in the privacy policy.
