15 small ways to feel close in a long-distance relationship

6 min read · OneSpace

Distance rarely breaks a relationship in one big moment. It wears it down in small ones: a missed call, a day you couldn’t share, a plan that drifted. The fix is also small — a handful of rituals you can keep up for months. Here are fifteen that couples actually stick to.

Respect each other’s clock

  1. Know the time there before you call. A quick glance saves a lot of “sorry, did I wake you?”
  2. Agree on one “golden hour” a day when you are both awake and free — and protect it.
  3. Send messages that don’t need an answer, like “thinking of you”. They land without pressure.

Share the boring parts

  1. Keep one shared list — groceries, errands, gifts. Seeing each other’s ordinary day is surprisingly intimate.
  2. Do a one-minute daily check-in: a mood and one honest line about your day.
  3. Swap a daily selfie. In a month you have a diary of the distance.

Always have the next date

  1. Never end a visit without the next one on the calendar, even if it’s tentative.
  2. Count it down together. Watching a number shrink is a real comfort on hard days.
  3. Plan the trip together — flights, packing, what you’ll do first. Planning is half the joy.

Make love tangible

  1. Write a letter that opens on a specific day: their birthday, their exam, the day they land.
  2. Keep a bucket list of places you’ll visit together, and add to it after every call.
  3. Watch the same film at the same time, then rate it separately and compare.

Protect the relationship from logistics

  1. Move decisions out of chat. Compare options in one place so calls can be about you.
  2. Remember the dates that matter — and the ones that matter only to the two of you.
  3. Say thank you for the small things. A “proud of you” travels further than you think.
