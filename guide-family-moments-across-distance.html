How to be part of a family celebration from another country

4 min read · OneSpace

Missing a family event hurts, especially when the people there matter to both partners. Remote presence works best when it starts before the video call: with a real contribution and something that lasts after the event.

Take ownership of one real task

  1. Research cake options, collect old photos or draft a toast while your partner handles local errands.
  2. Put deadlines and saved contacts next to the plan so nobody has to ask where things are.

Make the video moment count

  1. Agree on the time for a toast or short greeting, and keep it brief enough for the room.
  2. Ask for consent before sharing other people's photos outside the family.

Make a collaborative keepsake

  1. Build a shared scrapbook and choose a page design — both of you can add photos straight away.
  2. Export the finished pages to PDF for relatives who aren't using the app.
💡 OneSpace connection: shared occasions and checklists coordinate preparation; shared scrapbooks and PDF sharing make the memories portable.
