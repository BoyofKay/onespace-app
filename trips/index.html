One list per kind

Every app keeps its own list. OneSpace keeps one for both of you.

Five streaming services, four shops, three flight sites — each with its own list, and each of you with your own account. That is easily 20 lists between two people. Tap Share in any of them and OneSpace puts it in the one list where it belongs.

How OneSpace solves it

Four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Create the trip

    From, to, dates (or not yet), who is travelling, adults and children.

  2. 2

    Collect flight options

    Tap “Search on Google Flights” — OneSpace opens it with your route. Found one? Share it back to OneSpace, or paste the link.

  3. 3

    Compare and book

    Stops, baggage, seat, price, your preference stars — then mark one booked; it fills the trip dates and itinerary.

  4. 4

    Itinerary, documents, packing

    Stays, transport, activities and reservations in local time; PDFs and photos of tickets; a packing list split between you.

In detail

Everything it can do.

🛫

Flight comparison

Airline, airports, stops, cabin and checked bags, seat, price seen, notes and your preference — side by side.

🔁

To and from Google Flights

Open a pre-filled search, then share the result back — OneSpace reads airports, dates, airline and price.

🗺

Itinerary in local time

Flights, stays, transport, activities, bookings and notes — exactly as on the booking.

Private documents

Boarding passes, visas and bookings — only the two of you can open them.

Packing templates
