Step-by-step guides.

Short, practical how-tos for the things couples do most. Prefer to see it? Try the demo.

🚀 Getting started

Create your space and invite your partner
  1. Sign up with your email and accept the short terms.
  2. Create your space and give it a name.
  3. Share the invite code or show its QR code.
  4. Your partner signs up and taps “Scan your partner’s QR code”. That’s it — a space holds exactly two people.
Tell OneSpace how you live
  1. Tap “Tell OneSpace how you live” in your space.
  2. Choose living apart or together. Apart adds both cities’ clocks and weather to Today.
  3. Built-in gesture messages follow your choice — no “miles away” lines for couples at home.
Choose your language and theme
  1. Open Me and pick English or العربية — each of you chooses separately.
  2. Pick one of nine themes and a background you like.

☑️ Every day

Add anything in two taps
  1. Tap Quick add on Today.
  2. Choose reminder, to-do, event, note, trip, place, letter…
  3. Choose who it belongs to — you, your partner or both — and whether it is Shared or Only me.
See OneSpace events in your phone calendar
  1. Turn on “Show in my phone calendar” and allow calendar access.
  2. Shared events, trips, occasions and special dates appear in a separate “OneSpace” calendar in Google, Samsung or iPhone calendar.
  3. It is one way: change things in OneSpace, and OneSpace never reads your other calendars.
Send a little love
  1. Open A little love from Our space or Connect.
  2. Pick a gesture — hug, kiss, proud of you, call me…
  3. Keep the built-in message or write your own, then send.

🧭 Plan together

Decide together with an activity
  1. In Coordinate, create an activity — a sofa, a restaurant, a weekend away.
  2. Add options with links and prices; each of you marks what you like.
  3. Talk in the conversation (type @ to mention, or record a voice note), then record the decision.
  4. Use “Remind us” or add to the calendar — the items stay linked.
Plan a trip
  1. Create a trip in Coordinate → Trips.
  2. Found a flight? In Google Flights tap Share › OneSpace, or copy the link and paste it.
  3. Split the packing list and attach travel documents — only the two of you can open them.
Prepare a surprise occasion
  1. Create an occasion and set it to Only me.
  2. Add gift ideas with prices and a budget.
  3. Choose when to start preparing — OneSpace reminds you in time.

🔖 Remember

Count down (or up) to a special date
  1. Open Counters in Remember and add a date.
  2. For a past date, see both the time since the day itself and the next anniversary.
  3. Use “Link to another counter” to see the time between two moments.
Save a place from Google Maps
  1. In Google Maps tap Share › OneSpace (or copy the link into the place).
  2. Choose “Been there” or “Bucket list”, add photos, a date and a note.
  3. Open the map to see all your pins; tick bucket-list places to plan a trip.
Make a scrapbook
  1. Pick a template and add memories and photos.
  2. Invite your partner to add pages.
  3. Use “Save or share as PDF” to keep or print it.

🔒 Privacy & help

Keep something private
  1. When creating or editing an item, choose Only me instead of Shared.
  2. Only-me items never show on your partner’s phone.
Export or delete your data
  1. Open Me → Your data and account.
  2. Export first if you want a copy, then tap Delete my account.
  3. Locked out? Use the deletion request form.
Get help when you can’t sign in
  1. The sign-in screen has Help, Contact us and Report a problem — no account needed.
  2. Or use the website form; we reply by email.
